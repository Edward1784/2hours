How it works

Two calm hours, every morning

The scroll that starts in bed eats the best hours of your day. 2Hours puts it to sleep until you're really up.

1

Pick your traps

Choose the apps that pull you in with Apple's own Screen Time picker. 2Hours never sees which apps they are.

2

They sleep when you wake

At your wake time the apps lock for two hours. Open one and you meet Sunny instead of the feed. It works even when 2Hours is closed.

3

Get up to wake them

Walk 300 steps (brush your teeth, make coffee) or scan a barcode you registered in your bathroom. The lock lifts on its own.

Questions

Good to know

Is 2Hours free?

Yes. The morning lock, the bedtime lock and walking to unlock are free. 2Hours Pro is an optional subscription that adds unlimited apps, scan-to-unlock, Safari blocking and bigger step goals. It starts with a free trial; the price is shown in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel anytime in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Can I still use my phone during the lock?

Of course. Calls, messages, maps, music and every app you didn't pick work as usual. Only the apps you chose sleep.

What if I really need an app?

Get up and walk 300 steps, or scan your code. That's the point: a small action that wakes you up. The lock also ends on its own after two hours.

Does it work when 2Hours is closed?

Yes. 2Hours uses Apple's Screen Time framework, so iOS applies and removes the lock on schedule even if the app isn't running.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later. Screen Time permission is required to lock apps.
