Questions

Good to know

Can I still use my phone during the lock?

Of course. Calls, messages, maps, music and every app you didn't pick work as usual. Only the apps you chose sleep.

What if I really need an app?

Get up and walk 300 steps, or scan your code. That's the point: a small action that wakes you up. The lock also ends on its own after two hours.

Does it work when 2Hours is closed?

Yes. 2Hours uses Apple's Screen Time framework, so iOS applies and removes the lock on schedule even when the app isn't running.

Why does it need Screen Time and Motion access?

Screen Time lets iOS lock the apps you pick. Motion & Fitness counts your steps for the walk-to-unlock. Both stay on your iPhone. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone on iOS 16.4 or later.
