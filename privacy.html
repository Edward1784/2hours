Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

2Hours ("2Hours", "we", "us") is an iPhone app that locks the apps you choose for the first two hours after you wake up, and optionally at bedtime. It is made by Abderrahmane Gaci, the developer and data controller. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the personal parts stay on your iPhone, we use a little anonymous usage data to improve the app, and we never sell data or show ads.

1. Data that stays on your iPhone

This data is stored only on your device. We cannot see it and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

  • The apps and sites you lock. You choose them with Apple's Screen Time picker. iOS gives 2Hours an opaque token, not the names of the apps, and the lock is enforced by iOS itself.
  • Step counts. With your permission (Motion & Fitness), 2Hours reads your step count from your iPhone during an active lock, only to tell whether you've walked enough to unlock. Steps are not stored by us and never leave your device.
  • Camera and scan code. The camera is used only while you register or scan your unlock barcode. Images are not saved or transmitted; only the barcode value is kept on your device.
  • Your schedule, streaks and history, such as wake time, bedtime and the mornings you won. Some of it is also shown in the 2Hours widget, which reads it from the same device.

2. Data that leaves your iPhone

Anonymous usage analytics (Mixpanel)

To understand which parts of the app help people and where they get stuck, 2Hours sends usage events to our analytics provider, Mixpanel, Inc., using its EU data residency. Events are tied to a random identifier, not to your name, email or Apple ID. They include:

  • which screens you view and which buttons you tap, including onboarding answers (for example your wake time, bedtime, which unlock method you prefer, and how many apps you picked, but not which apps);
  • whether you started, cancelled or completed a purchase, and your plan status (free, trial, paid);
  • when a lock is lifted and how (steps or scan), and the minutes you got back;
  • technical details such as device model, iOS version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country or city) that Mixpanel derives from your IP address. Mixpanel does not store the IP address itself for this purpose.

On some installs, Mixpanel Session Replay records how the app's screens are used. All text and images are masked on your device before anything is sent, so replays never contain your step counts, scan codes or camera feed.

You can turn this off at any time in 2Hours › Settings › Share usage data. When it's off, no usage events or replays are sent.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive your card details or Apple ID. To know whether you have 2Hours Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives a random app user ID, your App Store purchase and subscription status, and a few app settings we attach to that ID (plan status, whether onboarding is done, your focus, wake time and bedtime, unlock method and current streak) so we can understand how subscribers use the app.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply.

3. What we don't do

  • No account, no sign-in, and no name, email or phone number required.
  • No advertising and no ad networks. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't use the advertising identifier.
  • We never sell or rent personal data, and never share Screen Time, step or camera data with anyone.
  • We don't collect which other apps are installed on your phone.

5. How long we keep data

On-device data stays until you delete the app or use Settings › Reset everything. Analytics and subscription records are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, and are deleted on request. Apple keeps purchase records under its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Stop analytics: Settings › Share usage data.
  • Revoke permissions: iPhone Settings › Screen Time, Privacy › Motion & Fitness, or Camera.
  • Delete on-device data: Settings › Reset everything, or delete the app.
  • Access, correction or deletion of data held by our providers: email us and we'll handle it within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you also have the right to object, restrict processing, data portability and to complain to your data protection authority. We rely on your consent and our legitimate interest in improving the app.

7. Children

2Hours is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
