1. What 2Hours is

2Hours is a digital wellbeing tool. Using Apple's Screen Time framework, it restricts the apps and websites you choose during the times you set, and lets you lift the restriction early by walking a number of steps or scanning a barcode you registered. It needs your Screen Time permission to work, and Motion & Fitness or camera permission for the unlock methods.

2. Please don't rely on 2Hours alone

2Hours helps you build a habit; it is not a medical, safety or parental-control product. iOS decides when restrictions apply, and things like iOS updates, low power states, changed permissions or a removed Screen Time authorization can stop a lock from working. Locks never block phone calls or emergency features, and you can always remove a restriction by changing your settings or deleting the app.
