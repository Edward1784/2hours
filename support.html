My apps didn't lock

  • Open 2Hours › Locks and check the morning lock is on for today, with your wake time right.
  • Make sure Screen Time permission is on: iPhone Settings › Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access › 2Hours.
  • Check you picked apps: 2Hours › Locks › Apps that sleep › Any app opens Apple's picker. iOS only locks what you select there.
  • Restart your iPhone once after first setup; iOS sometimes needs it to register schedules.

Steps aren't counting

  • Allow Motion & Fitness: iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness › 2Hours.
  • Steps count from the moment the lock starts, so carry your phone while you walk.
  • Can't walk right now? Finish a different mission instead, like Math or Shake.

Missions don't count my movement

Shake and Tilt read your iPhone's motion sensor while the mission is open, so keep 2Hours on screen.

Sunny patrol and morning sounds

Both use notifications, so allow them in iPhone Settings › Notifications › 2Hours. Morning sounds follow your ringer switch and Focus, and 2Hours doesn't replace your alarm clock. If you miss a patrol check for 5 minutes, your feeds go back to sleep until you finish another mission.

Scan-to-unlock

Register any barcode (shampoo, toothpaste, coffee) in 2Hours › Settings › Early unlock › Register a scan code (Pro). During a lock, scan the same code to lift it. Allow camera access if asked.

Restore a purchase

Open the 2Hours Pro screen and tap Restore, or 2Hours › Settings › Restore purchases, while signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Manage or cancel 2Hours Pro

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › 2Hours. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple handles them.

Delete my data

Use 2Hours › Settings › Reset everything, or delete the app. To also delete analytics and subscription records held by our providers, email us. Turn off analytics anytime in 2Hours › Settings › Share usage data. Details in the Privacy Policy.
